Project JOSH

Just an Open Source Humanoid

Meet JOSH, WE Bots' open-source humanoid robot platform. Our mission is to bring JOSH to life: a general-purpose robot that can move through its environment autonomously, balance reliably, and perform everyday movement tasks.

We're working toward an ambitious goal: Canada's first university-built, fully autonomous humanoid robot. As we grow the CHRC, we plan for JOSH to compete in its future seasons and at other robotics events.

About WE Bots

A place to build.
A future to shape.

WE Bots gives students the opportunity to build and contribute to a technology that is only beginning to find its place in the world: humanoid robotics. We're bringing Project JOSH to life while creating a competition for the next generation of university teams.

We envision a future where university humanoid and home-service robotics competitions are as prestigious and widespread as SAE competitions. We want Western University to help define that future.

Founded by WE Bots

Canadian Humanoid
Robotics Competition

Let's build something that lasts.

WE Bots is launching the CHRC: a new humanoid robotics competition for university and college teams, with its inaugural event planned for 2027.

The first season focuses on designing and building a humanoid hand. Future seasons will add more systems, building toward a full humanoid competition.

We want to give university teams a place to develop, test, and share their work, and build a competition that grows in scope, difficulty, and reputation year after year. WE Bots will be part of that journey as both the founding team and a competitor.
